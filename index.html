    A LITTLE LUCK GOES A LONG WAY ↗

    Let’s get rolling

    Two dice. One simple rule.

    Roll both dice and add the numbers. A total of 9 to 12 wins, 8 is a draw, and 2 to 7 is a miss. There are no stakes or accounts—just a small game of chance.

    1. Click Roll the dice or press Space. Each roll is independent, and every die face has the same chance.
    2. LIVE SESSION tracks rolls, wins, and your best streak of consecutive wins. A draw or miss breaks the streak.
    3. The history keeps your last eight rolls. Reset session clears all stats and history; reloading also starts fresh.

    Of the 36 possible pairs, 10 win, 5 draw, and 21 miss. That’s about a 28% chance of a win on every roll.

    Keyboard friendly. Reduced-motion preferences are respected.
